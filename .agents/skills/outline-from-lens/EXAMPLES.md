## Example 1 — Startup Growth

### Lens Output

```md
Recommended Lens:

People don't share solutions.
They share stories.

Everyone thinks:

Startups win by solving problems.

But:

Many products that solve problems still fail.

Because:

Utility gives people a reason to use something, not a reason to talk about it.

Which means:

People don't share solutions.
They share stories.
```

### Story Beats

```md
## Act 1 — Tension

Startup founders are taught to solve problems.

Yet many products that solve genuine problems still fail.

Something doesn't fit.

Reader reaction:

"That's strange."

## Act 2 — Investigation

Explore examples of useful products nobody talks about.

Contrast with products that spread through word-of-mouth.

Investigate why usefulness and growth appear disconnected.

Reader reaction:

"Now I see the pattern."

## Act 3 — Lens

Reveal:

People don't share solutions.
They share stories.

Utility gets adoption.
Story-worthiness gets distribution.

Reader reaction:

"I never thought about it that way."
```
