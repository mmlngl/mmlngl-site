### Category Shift

Does the lens move the discussion into a more useful domain?

Examples:

| Original Category  | New Category   |
| ------------------ | -------------- |
| Documentation      | Memory         |
| AI Training        | Food Supply    |
| Product Growth     | Storytelling   |
| Information Access | Nervous System |

Weak:

- Documentation is important.

Strong:

- Buildings have a memory problem.

Score: 1–10

---

#### Explanatory Power

Does the lens explain the observations?

Ask:

```text
If this lens is true,
what else does it explain?
```

Generate at least 10 additional examples.

Strong lenses naturally explain many phenomena.

Weak lenses explain only the original observation.

Example:

Lens:

- Buildings have a memory problem.

Explains:

- Lost plans
- Lost specifications
- Lost manuals
- Lost maintenance records
- Redrawing work
- Surveying costs
- Ownership handovers
- Planning submissions
- Contractor onboarding
- Facilities management

Score: 1–10

---

### Necessity vs Sufficiency Test

Many weak lenses confuse:

- Necessary conditions
- Sufficient conditions

Ask:

```text
Is this describing something that is required but not enough?
```

Examples:

Weak:

- Solving problems creates success.

Stronger:

- The startup world confuses necessity with sufficiency.

This test does not receive a score.

Use it to discover deeper reframes.

---

### 5. Generate alternatives

Generate at least 10 alternative lenses.

Generate across multiple domains:

- Biology
- Economics
- Storytelling
- Psychology
- Evolution
- Information Theory
- Systems Thinking

Do not stop after finding a reasonable lens.

The first lens is rarely the strongest lens.

---

## Expansion Test

If this lens is true, what else becomes true?

List at least 10 consequences, examples, or related phenomena.

If fewer than 5 emerge naturally:

- Generate alternative lenses
- Reclassify the problem
- Search for a stronger category shift

Strong lenses explain far more than the original observation.

---

## Anti-Patterns

### Clever But Doesn't Explain

Bad:

- Startup founders face a crisis of faith.

Why:

Memorable.

Explains very little.

A good lens increases explanatory power, not merely novelty.

## Golden Rule

The article is not the product.

- Create a category shift
- Become reusable mental shortcuts

The article merely earns the right to install the lens.
